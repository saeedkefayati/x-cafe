# Calibration Product A — Iced Caramel Latte

## Card Prompt

Create a premium editorial product photograph for a modern high-end café brand.

Product: Iced Caramel Latte.

The drink is served in a clean tall transparent cylindrical glass with realistic proportions.

Composition:
double espresso, cold whole milk, clear ice cubes, and a subtle caramel integration.

The milk and espresso should show a believable soft tonal transition rather than perfectly separated artificial layers.

Use restrained caramel streaks only where physically plausible.

Add realistic cold-glass condensation.

Ice must be transparent with believable refraction and natural irregularity.

Place the glass centrally on a matte warm light-travertine surface.

Background:
smooth warm oat-cream studio background, low saturation, very subtle gradient.

Camera:
eye-level with a subtle 70mm premium product-photography lens feeling and minimal perspective distortion.

Lighting:
large soft key light from upper-left, gentle fill, subtle natural edge definition, realistic soft contact shadow.

The image must feel minimal, calm, warm, crafted, contemporary, and premium.

No straw.
No spoon.
No coffee beans scattered around.
No text.
No logo.
No people.
No hands.
No watermark.
No decorative props.
No distorted glass.
No impossible ice geometry.

Aspect ratio 4:5.

The drink must remain clearly recognizable at ecommerce-card size.

---

## Hero Prompt

Create a cinematic premium editorial hero photograph of an Iced Caramel Latte for a high-end café website.

Use the exact same glass, lighting direction, lens feeling, milk/espresso realism, ice style, condensation, travertine surface, shadow softness, and visual language as the approved card image.

Position the product slightly right of center.

Leave generous clean negative space on the left for large website typography.

Background:
smooth warm oat-cream studio background with subtle natural tonal depth.

Avoid all unrelated props.

Aspect ratio 16:10.

# Calibration Acceptance Criteria

سه تصویر Card باید کنار هم بررسی شوند.

قبول فقط وقتی:

- نور از یک جهت و با یک softness است.
- Surface یک خانواده است.
- scale محصولات منطقی است.
- Backgroundها متفاوت ولی هم‌خانواده‌اند.
- تصاویر کنار هم شبیه یک Campaign هستند.
- Matcha neon نیست.
- Caramel Latte مصنوعی نیست.
- Cheesecake بیش از حد perfect نیست.
- هیچ geometry عجیب دیده نمی‌شود.
- Thumbnail readability خوب است.

بعد از قبولی، همین Style Bible برای کل Menu LOCK شود.
