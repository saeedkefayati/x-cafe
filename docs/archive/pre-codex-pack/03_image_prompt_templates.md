# A. Master Product Card Prompt

Create a premium editorial product photograph for a modern high-end café brand.

Product name: [PRODUCT_NAME]

Product type: [PRODUCT_TYPE]

Primary ingredients: [INGREDIENTS]

Important visual characteristics: [PRODUCT_DESCRIPTION]

Place the product prominently in the center of the frame with a clean, highly readable silhouette.

Use generous but controlled negative space.

Camera language must feel natural and consistent with premium commercial food photography, with a subtle 50–85mm product-photography lens feeling and minimal perspective distortion.

Place the product on [SURFACE_DESCRIPTION].

Use a smooth [BACKGROUND_DESCRIPTION] studio background with very subtle depth and a soft natural gradient.

Use a large soft key light from the upper-left side, gentle fill light, realistic soft shadow beneath the product, and subtle rim definition only where needed.

Preserve realistic food and drink textures, including relevant details such as crema, foam, condensation, ice, pastry crumb, chocolate, fruit, milk texture, or sauce.

The visual tone must feel premium, minimal, editorial, warm, crafted, contemporary, calm, and distinctive.

Avoid generic fast-food advertising.

Maintain the same camera language, lighting direction, shadow softness, product scale, background treatment, and visual density as the rest of the café product collection.

No text.
No logo.
No watermark.
No people.
No hands.
No unnecessary utensils.
No decorative clutter.
No distorted glass, ceramic, plate, or food geometry.
No unrealistic food structure.

Aspect ratio: 4:5.

The product must remain clearly recognizable at ecommerce-card size.
